This PRD was synthesised from saved-carts design notes that list rules but give no examples.
PASS if its Acceptance Examples turn the intent's decisions into concrete Given/When/Then examples: at least the 5-cart limit (a save refused at 5), repricing on restore, and guests being unable to save; AND the examples state what the shopper observes rather than UI mechanics (no clicking, buttons, fields or selectors).
FAIL if any of those three decisions has no example, or the examples script the UI.
