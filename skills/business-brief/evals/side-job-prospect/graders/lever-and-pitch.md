The user asked for a business brief on Kettlebird Coffee, a direct-to-consumer coffee
subscription store, before pitching a site-performance side job.

PASS if the reply does both:
1. Explicitly names what revenue depends on for this business (the lever): subscriber
   retention or repeat orders, and/or conversion (optionally times order value). Saying
   "subscription e-commerce" without naming what drives revenue is not enough.
2. Frames the performance pitch in the client's business terms (mobile conversion, lost
   orders, subscribers who can't pause or skip and cancel instead, Black Friday revenue), not
   only in technical terms (Lighthouse scores, bundle size, scripts).

FAIL if either is missing.
